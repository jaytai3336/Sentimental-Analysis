01. Methodology

Pipeline Architecture

Data Collection
Selenium scraping of Bloomberg articles and Trump tweets. Text preprocessing via spaCy & NLTK — tokenisation, lemmatisation, named entity recognition.
Sentiment Models
Rule-based: VADER. Traditional ML: SVM, Naive Bayes. Transformers: FinBERT, DistilBERT, Flair, RoBERTa. LSTM as sequence learner.
Topic Modelling
Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) for topic clustering, segmented by sentiment classification with interactive pyLDAvis visualisations.
Backtesting
Sentiment signals drive SPY long/short decisions, backtested against historical 2006–2025 OHLCV data. Strategy performance vs buy-and-hold comparison.
